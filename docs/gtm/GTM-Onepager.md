## Discovery Validation Test

**Every first call — Q3 ([03]):**

> *"Tell me about the last time you reconstructed what was true on a specific past date — regulator, opponent, or board. What did you do?"*

**Pass:** real incident + hours + manual work. **Fail:** no story / "latest version only" → not ICP.
