## FAQ

**1. How is this different from enterprise legal AI platforms?**  
Those tools optimize for Am Law scale: six-figure minimums, generic answers, limited explainability. DocMind is priced for mid-market teams and built around **provable citations**, **matter timelines**, and **trust scoring** — outputs you can attach to a board memo or disclosure schedule.

**2. Will you train models on my client data?**  
No. Your documents are processed for your queries only. We do not use customer content to train foundation models. Enterprise contracts include explicit zero-training clauses and sub-processor controls.

**3. What happens to my data if I cancel?**  
You export matters, citations, and audit logs (Business+). After 30 days, tenant data is deleted from active systems; backups purge within 90 days per our DPA.

**4. EU AI Act compliance — what exactly do you provide?**  
Deployer-facing documentation: risk classification worksheet, transparency notices template, human-oversight workflow map, provenance/confidence audit exports, and DPIA support materials. Enterprise includes review with our solutions team.

**5. Can we self-host?**  
Enterprise only — VPC or dedicated on-prem. Requires minimum term, security review, and custom SLA. Pro and Business are multi-tenant cloud (EU/UK/US residency on Enterprise).
