# DocMind OS — Customer Discovery Script

**Target persona:** In-house Senior Counsel / Head of Legal Ops, regulated mid-market (fintech / insurance / healthtech), EU/UK  
**Call length:** 30 min  
**Validation goal:** Confirm bitemporal pain as top-3 daily/weekly frustration  
**Anti-goal:** Do not pitch DocMind. Listen, do not sell.

---

## Pre-call Setup (1 min)

> "Thanks for the time. I'm not here to sell anything today — I'm trying to understand how teams like yours actually work. Thirty minutes, I'll mostly listen. Sound okay?"

> "Mind if I record for my notes? I won't share outside our team."

---

## Q1 — Warm-up / Context (3 min)

**Q:** "Walk me through a typical week. Where does most of your time go?"

**Listen for:**
- Volume signals (parallel matters, contracts, inquiries)
- Frustration markers ("I just…", "again…", "we still…")
- Hidden personas (who else is in the loop?)

**Follow-up probes:**
- "When you say 'review' — what does that mean step-by-step?"
- "How many people are usually involved?"

---

## Q2 — Document Reality (3 min)

**Q:** "For one matter or contract, how many documents are usually in play? Walk me through the last messy one."

**Listen for:**
- Multiple versions, amendments, superseding agreements
- "Somewhere in email", "someone had it", "I think latest version…"
- Document chaos as fact, not theory

**Follow-up:**
- "How do you know which version is currently in force?"
- "What happens when there are three amendments to one contract?"

---

## Q3 — The Time Question (4 min) ⚠️ VALIDATION CORNERSTONE

**Q:** "Tell me about the last time you had to reconstruct what was true on a specific date in the past — regulator question, opponent challenge, board ask. What did you actually do?"

*Not leading. Real story only. Do not mention dates-as-a-feature or any product.*

**Listen for — SUCCESS signals:**
- Specific incident they can name (recency = pain)
- Hours or days spent
- Manual reconstruction (Excel, Word redlines, email archaeology)
- Frustration or emotion
- "Luckily no one asked about X…"

**Listen for — KILL signals:**
- "Can't recall anything like that" → may not be ICP
- "We just use the latest version" → timeline may not be pain
- "Vendor X handles that" → possibly solved

**Follow-up probes:**
- "How long did that take?"
- "Who did the work? How many people?"
- "What was the worst part of that process?"
- "How many times in the last year?"

---

## Q4 — Quantification (2 min)

**Q:** "If you had to guess — how many hours per month does your team spend on those timeline-reconstruction tasks?"

**Listen for:**
- Numbers in hours, not "a lot"
- Whether they track at all (no measure = often underestimated)
- Per-matter vs team total

**Follow-up:**
- "If you count opportunity cost — what would those people do instead?"

---

## Q5 — Amendments / Supersedence (3 min)

**Q:** "How does your team track which contracts or policies are active, expired, or superseded — as of today?"

**Listen for:**
- Spreadsheets, manual logs, "Sharon knows"
- DMS stores files but not legal state
- Recent failure stories

**Follow-up:**
- "What happened the last time that process failed?"
- "How did you find the error?"

---

## Q6 — Current AI Tools (3 min)

**Q:** "What AI tools is your team testing or using on documents today? What's missing?"

**Listen for:**
- Trials (Harvey, CoCounsel, Copilot, Spellbook) → active search
- Failure modes ("made up citation", "doesn't know prior version")
- Budget signals ("tried X, didn't renew")

**Follow-up:**
- "What was the final straw on not buying / not renewing?"
- "What would need to be true for you to look at AI again?"

---

## Q7 — Citations & Trust (3 min)

**Q:** "Imagine AI answered a hard legal question for you. What would you need to see before you'd forward it to a client or regulator?"

**Listen for:**
- Source linking demand
- Page vs paragraph vs exact-passage requirements
- "Could I use this in court?" mindset
- Desire to know how confident the system is

**Follow-up:**
- "Have you hit a hallucinated citation in an AI tool? What happened?"

---

## Q8 — Workflow Future (2 min)

**Q:** "If you had one magic wand for your workflow — one thing you'd change tomorrow — what is it?"

**Listen for:**
- Whether timeline / "what was true when" appears in top 3
- Other pains (broader roadmap)
- Vague answer → possibly shallow pain

---

## Q9 — Buying Process (3 min)

**Q:** "If your team decided to add a new legal AI tool, what happens from first idea to signed contract? Who's involved?"

**Listen for:**
- Champion (them? GC? Legal Ops?)
- Economic buyer (CFO / CLO?)
- Blocker (IT / Security / DPO?)
- Security review, DPA, budget cycle
- Timeline (3 / 6 / 9 months?)

**Follow-up:**
- "How long did your last legal tech purchase take?"
- "What was the hardest step?"

---

## Q10 — The Reveal + Ask (3 min)

*First time you describe the product. Max 30 seconds.*

> "Quick context on what we're building: an AI tool focused on reconstructing document state as of any date, with citations to the exact passage in the source file, and explicit confidence scoring on claims. Not magic — engineering around temporal logic and provenance.  
> **Question:** How much does that map to pain you actually have, 1–10?"

**Listen for:**
- **9–10 + specifics** → strong validation; ask for pilot path
- **6–8 + specifics** → partial fit; dig into gaps
- **≤5** → wrong ICP or wrong pain

**Final ask:**
> "Can I come back in two weeks with a tighter demo? And do you know one or two peers in a similar role I should talk to?"

## Post-call Scorecard

Fill immediately after the call:

| Signal | Score 1–5 | Evidence (quote) |
|--------|:---------:|------------------|
| Bitemporal pain in Q3 without prompting | | |
| Can quantify hours (Q4) | | |
| Active AI tool trial (Q6) | | |
| Champion energy (Q9: they'd push internally) | | |
| Q10 score ≥ 7 | | |
| Gave ≥ 1 referral | | |

**Total interpretation:**

| Score | Action |
|:-----:|--------|
| **24–30** | Hot. Book demo within 5 days. |
| **17–23** | Warm. Tailored case study. Re-touch in 2 weeks. |
| **10–16** | ICP unclear. Second discovery OR de-prioritize. |
| **< 10** | Not ICP. Close politely. |

---

## Facilitator Reminders

- "That's interesting" is not validation — ask for the last time it happened.
- No budget movement, no internal push, no referral = weak signal regardless of enthusiasm.
- Never ask "Would it be valuable if…" before Q10.
- If they pitch *your* solution back to you before Q10, redirect: "Help me understand what you did last time instead."
